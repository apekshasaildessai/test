Regulatory Risk

Changes in SEBI guidelines, taxation, or government regulations

Business Risk

Higher operational/financial risks in SMEs and early-stage companies

Concentration Risk

Potential for concentrated exposure in select sectors/companies

Valuation Risk

Uncertainties in pre-IPO valuations due to limited comparables
